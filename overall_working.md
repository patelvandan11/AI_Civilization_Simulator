## 1. Core Philosophy & Design Vision

The simulator models a living, autonomous micro-nation colony set in **Navsari, Gujarat, India** (`20.9467° N, 72.9520° E`). Every citizen has an identity, family relationships, occupation, home address, vehicle, schedule, daily budget, and nutritional requirements.

The simulation runs continuously in real-time or at accelerated warp speeds (`10×, 60×, 1000×`), generating emergent civic events, dynamic supply-and-demand markets, traffic flows on roads, and real-time live news bulletins.

## 2. Timeline, Clock & Calendar Mechanics

- **24-Hour Indian Standard Time (IST)**: The simulation clock displays time in 24-hr IST notation (e.g. `08:30 hrs (IST)`).
- **Indian Date Conversion**: Seamlessly projects cumulative game days into standard Indian calendar dates starting from `01/01/2026` (e.g. `19/08/2026`).
- **Clock Ratio**:
  - `1 In-Game Hour = 60 Seconds` (1 minute real-time at 1×).
  - `1 In-Game Day = 1440 Seconds` (24 minutes real-time at 1×).
- **Speed Multipliers**:
  - `⏸ PAUSE`: Halts simulation time while allowing administrative configuration.
  - `1×`: Real-time standard pacing.
  - `10×`: 1 in-game hour every 6 real seconds.
  - `60×`: 1 in-game hour every 1 real second.
  - `1000×`: High-speed historical warp.

| **00:00 - 08:00** | 🌙 Night Rest | Sleeping in residence beds; town night curfew active. |

| **19:00 - 22:00** | 🍲 Family Dinner | Kitchens prepare and consume fresh evening dinner; streetlamps turn on. |
| **22:00 - 00:00** | 💤 Evening Leisure & Rest | Reading, studying, or resting before night sleep. |

## 4. Agricultural Cultivation & Kisan LangChain AI Agent

- **12 Cultivation Plots**: Grow crops including Wheat, Carrots, Sweet Corn, Broccoli, Cabbage, Cucumbers, Red Chilis, Strawberries, Apples, Watermelons, Bananas, and Pumpkins.
- **Growth Engine**: Accurate progress percentage and remaining seconds that scale with time acceleration.
- **Kisan Seed Market Depot**: Buy certified seeds in single or 5x bulk packs, or plant across all empty soil plots in one click.
- **Autonomous Kisan AI Agent**:
  - Automatically audits plot readiness and barn stocks.
  - Harvests mature crops.
  - Auto-procures missing seed stocks and plants empty soil plots prioritized by deficit.

## 5. Industrial Revolution, Petroleum Cracking & Coastal Shipyards

### Sector 1: Navsari Coastal Shipyard & Maritime Docks
- Harbor master oversees cargo ships, passenger ferries, and fishing trawlers.
- Ships consume marine bunker fuel to embark on commercial freight voyages, bringing deep sea fish harvests and export income.

### Sector 2: Gulf Oil Refinery & Distillation Complex
- Extracts crude petroleum and cracks it into high-octane petrol, diesel, and marine bunker fuel.
- Automated supply chain channels refined petrol directly to retail pumps.

### Sector 3: Highway 48 Petrol & EV Superstation
- Automatically refuels traveling citizen cars, scooters, freight trucks, and tractors.
- Station collects revenue, and credits **10% civic fuel tax** directly to the municipal treasury.

### Sector 4: Heavy Foundry & Steel Smelting Works
- Operates industrial blast furnaces converting raw iron ore into structural steel beams used for civic bridges, shipyard berths, and municipal highways.

## 7. Civic Governance, Democratic Elections & Cabinet PMO

- **Democratic Elections**: Every 10 in-game years (10 in-game days), citizens cast ballots to elect:
  - **Prime Minister (PM)**: Directs macro-economic development and tax relief.
  - **District Magistrate (DM)**: Regulates civic compliance and treasury balance.
  - **Finance Minister**: Audits corporate and citizen taxes; approves welfare subsidy checks for impoverished families.
  - **Education Minister**: Manages community schooling and teacher curriculum.
  - **Infrastructure Minister**: Commissions civic public works (School, General Hospital, Central Park, Highway 48 Expansion).
- **Automated Welfare Net**: Families with budgets below the poverty threshold ($15) receive automated government subsidies from the city treasury.

## 9. Audio Experience & Dual-Mode Cozy Music

1. **Custom MP3 Music Track**:

   - The simulator automatically loops your track with smooth fade-in and volume controls.
2. **Procedural Lofi Synthesizer**:
   - If no MP3 is added, clicking **`🎵 Cozy Tunes`** runs a built-in Web Audio ambient synthesizer featuring warm lowpass-filtered piano pads (`Cmaj7`, `Am7`, `Fmaj7`, `G7`) and gentle Kalimba arpeggio melodies.
3. **Procedural Wooden Clicks & Chimes**:
   - Tactile wooden click feedback on all buttons with harmonious pentatonic success chimes on actions.
