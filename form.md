# 📝 AI Civilization Simulator — User Review & Evaluation Form

Welcome to the **User Feedback & Playtester Review Form** for the **Navsari AI Civilization Simulator**.

This document provides a structured framework to collect qualitative ratings, performance diagnostics, feature requests, and gameplay impressions from multiple citizens, playtesters, and administrators.

## 📊 Summary Review Rubric (1 to 5 Stars)

| Rating | Definition |

## 📋 Evaluation Questionnaire Categories

### 1. 🎨 Visual Aesthetics & Day/Night Mode
- How visually pleasing and readable is the **Cozy Day Mode** (warm linen, amber parchment, cream cards)?
- How immersive is the **Night Mode** (midnight obsidian, neon amber/emerald glow)?
- Is the text contrast and typography clear on all dialogs and modals?

### 2. 🎵 Soundscape & Audio Experience
- Are the wooden UI clicks and harmonic chimes satisfying and non-intrusive?
- How is the generative **Cozy Tunes** synthesizer (Kalimba + Piano pads)?
- If tested, how smoothly does your custom MP3 track (`public/audio/cozy_tunes.mp3`) loop?

### 3. ⚡ Simulation Timing & Multi-Speed Engine (1× / 10× / 60× / 1000×)
- Does the in-game clock (24-hr IST & Indian calendar) advance accurately?
- How responsive is the simulation when speeding up to **10× Fast** or **60× Speed**?
- Do crops mature, news feeds update, and citizen routines accelerate smoothly?

### 4. 🌾 Agriculture & Autonomous Kisan AI Agent
- How intuitive is planting seeds from the **Kisan Seed Market Depot** and harvesting crops?
- Does the **Autonomous Kisan Farming Agent** effectively audit deficits and replenish barns?

### 5. 🏭 Heavy Industry & Energy Supply Chain
- How engaging are the 4 industrial sectors (Shipyard, Oil Refinery, Highway 48 Superstation, Heavy Foundry)?
- Is the automated petroleum distillation and vehicle refueling log clear and balanced?

### 6. 🗺️ Geospatial Satellite Map & Citizen Commutes
- Do the citizen vehicle markers (🚗 Cars, 🛵 Scooters, 🚜 Tractors) accurately follow roads on the Navsari satellite map?
- Is the daily routine (Breakfast -> Commute -> Work -> Grocery Shopping -> Dinner -> Sleep) realistic?

### 7. 🏛️ Democratic Governance & PMO Cabinet
- How responsive are the 10-year democratic elections and cabinet tax adjustments?
- Is the automated social welfare safety net functioning properly for low-budget families?
